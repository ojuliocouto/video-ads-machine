# Example annotated script. One scene per line, in video order.
#
# Format:  [visual instruction] the words you say in this scene
#
# The text AFTER the bracket is the narration: it is exactly what you
# recorded, and the captions are generated from it word by word.
# The text INSIDE the bracket is the visual direction.
#
# Scene types come from words in the instruction:
#   presenter / avatar   -> you full-screen (add "zoom" for a slow zoom-in)
#   lettering            -> a big highlighted word on screen
#   lettering + logo     -> big word plus your logo
#   logo                 -> your logo only
#   anything else        -> a B-roll insert (needs an inserts.json entry;
#                           never put "presenter"/"avatar" in an insert line)
#
# In lettering scenes:  KEY = the giant highlighted word,
#                       LEAD = the small italic line above it.
#
# This example uses only presenter and lettering scenes on purpose, so it
# builds with just your voice recording (no B-roll files needed). Blank lines
# and lines without a leading [bracket] are ignored, so notes are fine.

[presenter, slight zoom] Você ainda faz tudo isso na mão, gastando o seu tempo?
[presenter] Eu montei um jeito de resolver isso em três passos simples.
[lettering | LEAD: um método de | KEY: 3 PASSOS] E qualquer pessoa consegue aplicar.
[presenter, slight zoom] Sem experiência, sem equipe, sem complicação.
[lettering + logo | LEAD: toque no botão | KEY: COMECE HOJE] Toca no botão aqui embaixo e garante a sua vaga.
